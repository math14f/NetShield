Privacy Policy for NetShield
Last Updated: 21. Juni 2026

NetShield ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed to be a secure, local-first web filtering tool.

Data Collection
NetShield does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or user activity.

Specifically:

We do not track your browsing history.
We do not log which sites are blocked or allowed.
We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, or IP address.
Local Analysis: All content and iframe analysis (checking for games, proxies, or canvas elements) is performed entirely locally within your browser. No web page content is ever analyzed or stored outside your device.
The extension does not communicate with any external servers, except to fetch remote blocklists and allowlists from URLs defined by you or your administrator. This communication does not include any of your personal data.

How We Use Permissions
declarativeNetRequest / declarativeNetRequestFeedback: Used to apply blocking rules and receive feedback on blocked requests locally without ever seeing your web traffic.
storage: Used to save administrator-defined settings and local configurations.
alarms: Used to periodically refresh the blocking and allowance rules (e.g. golden hours/break times).
tabs: Used to inspect the top-level URL of the active tab to ensure whitelist protection for school portals, and to redirect blocked tabs to the built-in blocked.html page.
notifications: Used to display local desktop notifications to the user (e.g. warning the user 5 minutes before a break ends).
host_permissions (<all_urls>): Required for the filtering rules and local content scanners to apply to websites.
